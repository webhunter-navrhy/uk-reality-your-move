About the property

Description property

Ideal three bedroom semi detached family home, off Dalston Road, West Carlisle. The accommodation offers entrance hallway, living room, kitchen, dining room and conservatory. The first floor has three bedrooms and bathroom. Externally is a low maintenance front garden, driveway, garage and rear garden with decked area.

Situation

Situated between Wigton Road and Dalston Road. Convenient location within walking distance to local amenities, Cumberland Infirmary, Caldew Lea and St Bede's Primary Schools, supermarkets. City Centre is just over one mile away. Close to regular bus route.
