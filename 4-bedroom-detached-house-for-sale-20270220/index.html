16 Rivermead, Lincoln, LN6 8FB

A Substantial Four-Bedroom Detached Family Home with Games Room, Double Garage & Enclosed Garden

An impressive, turn-key family home offering generous accommodation, excellent flexibility and superb entertaining space, positioned within a desirable cul-de-sac setting.

Welcome to 16 Rivermead, a substantial detached home offering the space, versatility and practicality that modern family life demands.

Presented in turn-key condition, this impressive property is ready for its new owners to move straight in and enjoy. With four generous double bedrooms, multiple reception and working-from-home spaces, a well-appointed kitchen-diner, utility room and excellent additional accommodation, the property offers an exceptional combination of comfort and flexibility.

One of the home's particular highlights is the double garage, approximately half of which has been converted to create a dedicated games room. This versatile space provides an ideal setting for family entertaining, children's activities, a home cinema, gym or additional leisure space, while retaining valuable garage and storage provision.

The accommodation

The welcoming entrance leads into a home designed with both everyday family life and entertaining in mind.

The generous living room provides a comfortable principal reception space, while the separate study offers an ideal dedicated home-working environment or could readily be adapted to suit individual requirements.

At the heart of the property is the kitchen-diner, creating a sociable space for family meals, entertaining friends and everyday living. A separate utility room adds valuable practicality, keeping household chores conveniently away from the main living areas. A ground-floor WC completes the principal downstairs accommodation.

Upstairs, there are four double bedrooms, providing genuinely versatile sleeping accommodation rarely found in modern family homes.

The principal bedroom benefits from its own en-suite, creating a private retreat away from the remainder of the household. The remaining bedrooms are served by a generous four-piece family bathroom.

Outside

The property occupies an appealing cul-de-sac position, providing a quieter setting while retaining excellent accessibility.

To the front, there is substantial off-road parking, together with the double garage.

The garage has been cleverly adapted, with approximately 50% converted into a games room, creating an exciting additional space with a multitude of possible uses. The remaining garage area continues to provide useful storage and parking provision.

To the rear is an enclosed garden, providing a private outdoor area for relaxation, entertaining and family use. A courtyard area to the side provides an additional outdoor space and further enhances the property's versatility.

A home designed for modern family life

With its substantial proportions, four double bedrooms and flexible ground-floor accommodation, 16 Rivermead offers considerably more than a conventional four-bedroom detached house.

The combination of a dedicated study, kitchen-diner, utility, games room, double garage, generous parking and enclosed garden makes this a particularly versatile property for a growing family or anyone looking for space to work, entertain and relax under one roof.

Presented in turn-key condition, there is little left to do other than move in, unpack and make the property your own.
